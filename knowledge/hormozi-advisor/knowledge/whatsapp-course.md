# WhatsApp Lead Qualification Course
## Matt's Framework (developed with Claude)

---

## Core Philosophy
> "You are not building a sales sequence. You are building a trust sequence that happens to qualify the lead."

WhatsApp is CHAT mode. Not reading mode. Not email mode. Not sales page mode.

---

## The 5 Principles

1. **First message = short + one question.** The only goal of message #1 is to get message #2. Nothing else.

2. **The question must feel like curiosity, not interrogation.** If it feels like a form, they'll ghost you.

3. **Completeness is a trap.** Don't explain everything upfront. Leave room for them to ask.

4. **Progressive qualification.** Move from easy → sensitive. Never open with budget.

5. **WhatsApp ≠ email.** Short. Casual. One idea per message.

---

## The Intro Message Formula

**Structure:** [context hook] + [one curiosity question]

Bad: "Hi! I'm a real estate agent at Bali Exception Estate and I specialize in villas in Bali and Lombok. I'd love to tell you about our listings. Are you looking to buy?"

Good: "Hey [name] — saw you were asking about Canggu villas. Still looking or you found something?"

Rule: Under 2 lines. One question. Sounds like a person, not a pitch.

---

## The Follow-Up Sequence

- **Message 2:** React to their answer. Ask one more question that goes slightly deeper.
- **Message 3:** Show you understand their situation. Offer one specific insight or option.
- **Message 4:** Only now can you move toward logistics (budget framing, timeline, viewing).

Total messages before closing loop or qualifying: 4-6. Not 1. Not 10.

---

## Budget Framing (Sensitive Topic Approach)

**Never use the word "budget."** Budget implies ceiling. It sounds like you're figuring out how much to extract.

Instead: *"What are you comfortable investing?"*

This framing:
- Implies agency (they choose)
- Removes ceiling connotation
- Feels collaborative, not transactional

Timing: only ask after you've established enough trust (usually message 4-5 minimum).

---

## The Validation Toolkit

Before sending any WhatsApp message, check:
- [ ] Is this under 3 lines?
- [ ] Does it contain exactly one question?
- [ ] Does the question feel like curiosity or like a form field?
- [ ] Am I moving forward or creating friction?
- [ ] Would I send this to a friend? (tone check)
