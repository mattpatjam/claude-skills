# Cold Outreach Playbook
## Source: acquisition.com Leads Course — Cold Outreach Module

---

## What It Is

Privately contacting strangers one-on-one to tell them about what you sell.
Channels: email, phone call, DM, SMS, voicemail, Direct Mail, handwritten card.

Cold outreach builds on warm outreach — master warm first. The same principles apply, just executed with more precision because strangers give you seconds, not minutes.

---

## The 3 Problems Strangers Create (and how to solve each)

### Problem 1: You have no way to contact them
**Solution — Build a list.** Three methods:

| Method | How | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **Scrape** | Lead scraping software (search "outbound lead scraping tool") | Easy, but competitors use same lists — more saturated |
| **Buy** | List brokers — give them your audience criteria, they send a sample first | Medium effort, can be more nuanced than software |
| **Elbow grease** | Join interest groups/communities, manually identify qualifying members, contact them outside the platform | Hard, but the most virgin lists — people haven't been hammered |

Action: subscribe to multiple scraping tools, test a few hundred from each, find what's responsive, scale what works.

Interest groups = warmest cold audience. Members are already problem-aware. They're looking for a solution — you intercept them.

### Problem 2: They ignore you
**Solution — Personalize + big fast value.**

**Personalization:**
- Research each lead before messaging — collect one specific snippet about them
- Include that snippet in every cold call, email, DM
- Make cold outreach feel like a friend reaching out
- Match area code on phone calls (higher pickup rate)
- Use their name + something specific = buy yourself more seconds

**Writing level:**
- Rewrite all scripts below 3rd grade reading level
- Hormozi got a 50% response bump just from simplifying language
- If they hit one word they don't understand, they stop reading

**Big fast value:**
- Offer something other people charge for — for free
- Don't tickle interest, blow their mind in 30 seconds
- Your lead magnet needs to be so good it feels stupid to say no
- Tripled response rates at Gym Launch by switching the free offer to something competitors were charging for

**Script format:**
- Phone/chat scripts: 1-2 pages max
- Emails: half a page max
- First DM: 2-3 sentences
- Talk about them, not you

### Problem 3: Not enough chances to tell people about your stuff
**Solution — Increase volume via 3 levers:**

**1. Automate delivery** (what you create)
- Live: personal voice memo per lead, handwritten card, live call — highest response rate, most time
- Recorded: templated email/text/voicemail blast — lower response, massive scale
- Rule: fewer leads available = more personalization. More leads available = more automation.

**2. Automate distribution** (how it gets sent)
- Manual: dial each number, click send on each email
- Automated: blast 1,000 emails/texts/voicemails at once
- Target: max personalization + max volume (tech makes this increasingly possible)

**3. Follow up more times in more ways**
- More ways: phone + email + DM — different people respond to different channels
- More times: contact quickly, 2-3x in the first few days, then 1x/day for 7 days
- Multiple attempts signal importance → curiosity rises → FOMO kicks in
- After full sequence: wait 3-6 months, then restart the list from the top
- Reasons people don't respond: didn't see it, bad timing, circumstances changed — all three justify following up again

## Benchmarks

**Cold calls:**
- 100 calls/day
- 25% pickup rate = 25 pickups
- 25% take the lead magnet = ~6 takers
- ~4 become engaged leads
- = 1 engaged lead per hour of calling

**Cold email (automated, portfolio company example):**
- 2,000 emails/day (1 VA with multiple tools)
- 30% open rate
- 10% open-to-reply rate
- 40 engaged leads/day
- 10% close rate = 4 customers/day
- At $20K LTV: ~$80K revenue per day from cold email alone

**ROI benchmark:**
- Target ≥3:1 — make at least 3x the lifetime gross profit of a customer vs. what it cost to acquire them
- To get the real cost, add up every labor + software expense across all three steps (list building, personalization/scripting, volume/automation) — cold outreach is labor-intensive and easy to underprice if you only count software spend
- Most people underestimate both the volume and the time it takes to see this ratio pay off, then quit early and call the method broken (same failure mode as the rep-management note above: benchmark against someone who's already made it work, not against your first week's numbers)

## Voicemail Script That Gets Callbacks

> "Hey [Name], it's [First Name] — calling in reference to [competitor they'd recognize]. Give me a call back at [number]."

Why it works:
- Short — no overthinking
- Relevant — competitor name triggers curiosity
- Opens a loop — humans hate unresolved loops, they call back
- Sounds like someone who knows them

What NOT to do: don't say "this is [first name] from [company]" — it flags you as a cold caller immediately.

## Managing Reps (When You Scale Beyond Yourself)

- Give each rep 70% of their max lead capacity — forces them to work each lead properly
- Same leads per rep = fair absolute quotas (e.g., 70 leads in = 7 appointments out)
- Getting your own leads first is critical — you understand how hard they are to get, so you won't waste them
- New to outreach: find the best rep, copy what they do exactly, do 2x their volume. Don't change anything until you beat their numbers.

## Why Bother With Cold Outreach

1. **No content needed** — business doesn't depend on a face or social following
2. **Invisible to competitors** — everything is private, no one can copy you
3. **Predictable machine** — once one person converts end-to-end, it's just a volume game
4. **Pipeline stacks** — leads from 6-18 months ago keep converting over time
5. **Platform-proof** — cold call laws and email haven't materially changed in decades
6. **Less compliance pain** — only legal compliance, no platform bans or algorithm changes
7. **Makes the business more sellable** — an acquirer sees a scalable system, not a personal brand
8. **Hard to copy** — done in private, no one sees your scripts or angles

---

## The Formula

```
Output = People reached × Response rate
```

You can move either variable. The goal is to move both.
Volume negates luck. Enough reps makes skill learnable twice as fast.

## Action Checklist

- [ ] Pick list-building method (scrape → buy → elbow grease, or all three)
- [ ] Define exact audience criteria
- [ ] Test sample of 200-300 leads before scaling any source
- [ ] Write opening script: personalized, below 3rd grade reading level, 2-3 sentences
- [ ] Define lead magnet — something others charge for, offered free
- [ ] Set daily volume target (minimum 100 outreach attempts/day)
- [ ] Build follow-up sequence: 2-3x day 1, 1x/day days 2-7
- [ ] After full sequence: wait 3-6 months, recycle list

The only business where the target audience is genuinely cold (other real estate agencies, not Matthieu's personal network).

- **List:** Sales Navigator or RE agency directories/brokers, filtered to agencies still running manual follow-up and content
- **Big fast value:** swap a generic "strategy call" for a free 30-day automation pilot — same lever that 3x'd take rates in the Gym Launch case study (`leads.md`): give away something competitors charge for, don't tickle interest
- **When:** viable now, in parallel with the STCC warm-referral path — cold needs its own list and script, not a fallback for warm running dry

### Bali Exception — true cold, distinct from CRM reactivation

CRM re-activation of past/gone-quiet leads is warm-recycle (already covered in `leads.md`) — this is the actual stranger-cold play.

- **List:** Bali property-buyer interest groups (Facebook/Telegram) — the "warmest cold audience," members already problem-aware
- **Personalization:** reference their specific post/comment before pitching anything
- **Big fast value:** free listing-fit consult (does this property/budget make sense for what you're describing)

### Lombok Land Development / Bali Real Estate Fund — cold investor list-building

Closes the $150-250K funding gap with Swiss/EU HNW individuals outside the personal network.

- **List:** investor interest groups/forums, or list brokers specializing in accredited/HNW investors
- **Big fast value:** the investor pitch deck itself is the lead magnet — send a short personalized teaser first, full deck on response
- **Note:** same list-building effort doubles for the early-stage Bali Real Estate Fund raise — investor audience overlaps, no need to build two lists
