# Copywriting

*Sources: Sabri Suby*

---

## Core Principle

Copy is messaging your market reads or consumes — written or video. Its job is to move a prospect from where they are now to where they want to go, and to make them want to take the next step with you.

The first transaction in any business is when a prospect gives you their name and email. Everything before that is copy. Everything after that is still copy.

---

## The NEUE Wave Formula

Every piece of copy — ads, VSLs, landing pages, emails, product pages — must signal all six of these:

| Letter | Quality | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| N | **New** | This is novel. Something worthy of attention. Scratches the shiny-object instinct. |
| U | **Unique** | Unlike anything your market has seen before. |
| E | **Exciting** | A huge opportunity that has only become possible now. |
| W | **Easy** | No implied heavy work to get the benefit. If there's perceived effort, people disengage. |
| A | **Predictable** | Proof that others have already gotten this result. Parades of evidence. Parade of proof. |
| E | **Huge** | The benefit or opportunity must be enormous. Small benefits get drowned out by everything competing for attention. |

**On polarization:** If your copy is truly NEUE Wave, it will be polarizing. That is expected and correct. If nobody is objecting, your copy is too vanilla. A target: if less than 50% of comments are negative, the message probably isn't bold enough. Messages that spark debate get rewarded by algorithms.

## The 9 Questions Every Sales Message Must Answer

Applies to: Google Ads, Facebook Ads, VSLs, landing pages, product pages, home pages, email sequences — any message asking a prospect to take action.

If you don't answer all nine, you will not convert as much as you could.

### Question 1: Is this of personal interest to me?
Your headline must speak directly to your dream buyer — not a group, one specific person. Include a big specific benefit that person wants more than anything, wrapped in intrigue.

Lazy but reliable tactic: call out your audience explicitly.
- "Attention: 42-year-old men with 12 kilos to lose"
- "Attention: business owners who want more customers"

Put who you're talking to in the eyebrow/subheadline before the main headline.

### Question 2: Why should I pay attention to this RIGHT NOW?
In a world of infinite distraction, why this, why now? If there's no time sensitivity, the prospect delays. Delay is the death of the sale.

You must **sell the consumption of the ad itself** — don't assume they'll read to the end. Give them a reason to keep going from the first line.

### Question 3: Is this the exact specific solution I need?
Make it feel custom-made. The prospect should feel like you've been reading their emails — that this offer was built specifically for their problem, not a general solution for everyone.

Show how your offer solves their specific problem differently from the alternatives they've already tried (and that have failed them).

### Question 4: What is NEUE Wave about this?
See NEUE Wave framework above. The offer must be new, unique, exciting, easy, predictable, and huge.

### Question 5: What is the undeniable proof?
Do not say it. Show it. Prove it like you're convincing a jury — leave zero room for reasonable doubt.

- Screenshots of results
- Before/after
- Client reviews and testimonials
- Average results across your customer base
- Third-party validation

Build a folder called "proof" and drop every piece of evidence in it as you collect it. The more undeniable the proof, the more powerful the message.

## Long-Form Copy Principles

Long copy works — but only if it's compelling, not just long.

- **Every sentence must earn the next one.** The reader should be on the edge of their seat, wanting the next word.
- **Map the journey:** Where is your prospect right now? Where do they want to go? Your copy must bridge that gap.
- **Value first.** The ad should deliver value even if the prospect doesn't convert. If they watch and don't buy, they should still have learned something. That's what earns trust.
- **The VSL structure:** Call out the problem → educate about all existing solutions and how they fall short → pivot to your offer as the logical conclusion. Everything leading up to the offer is informational.

## The Lead Magnet / LIVO

*(Confirmed across multiple transcripts — see also `marketing-strategy.md`)*

The best lead magnet ever created: a **down-and-dirty PDF** (or equivalent).

Why it works:
- Tangible solution to a specific, burning problem
- Gives an instant result without requiring a sales call
- Information inside should be better than what competitors charge for in paid courses
- Prospect consumes it, gets disproportionate value, and the next logical step is to reach out

**Key principle:** Value is the antidote to skepticism. When you give so much value upfront for free, you answer the biggest sales objection before the prospect even has it: "Are you legit?"

If the free content is better than what competitors charge for, the prospect is already sold by the time they speak to your team.

## Copywriting Rules

*(Confirmed across multiple transcripts — see also `facebook-ads.md`)*

- Write at a 5th grade reading level
- Short sentences, short paragraphs
- 1-2 syllable words
- Write to ONE person ("you / your") — not a group
- Specificity beats generality every time: "92 leads/month" vs "more leads"
- Sell the click / the consumption — not the product
- Don't make ads look like ads
- Add personality and specificity — generic = doubt
