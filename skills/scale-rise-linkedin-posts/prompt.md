## POST FORMAT & STRUCTURE

Same mechanical rules as the real estate skill:

### CHARACTER COUNT
Target: 1,200 to 1,600 characters. Never exceed 1,600. Never go below 1,000.

### THE HOOK (Lines 1-2)
- Under 200 characters
- A surprising stat, a bold claim, or a painful question the reader recognizes
- Creates an open loop
- No emojis in the first line, no "excited to announce," no throat-clearing

Strong hook examples:
"Most B2B service businesses lose leads in the first 48 hours, not from bad marketing."
"A service business doesn't need more leads. It needs to stop losing the ones it already has."
"I turned down a client this month. Here's why."

Weak hook examples (never do these):
"Today I want to talk about AI in real estate."
"As someone building an AI automation business..."
"In today's fast-moving world of AI..."

### THE BODY
- No paragraph longer than 3 lines
- One idea per paragraph
- Bullets/numbered lists for 3+ items, max 5 per list
- Exactly one specific, verified data point or concrete example
- Active voice throughout
- "How I" beats "How to" — anchor in Matthieu's actual experience building Scale-Rise or
  working with a client, not generic AI commentary

Avoid these words/phrases entirely: "Exciting news", "Thrilled to announce", "Game-changer",
"Synergy", "In today's fast-paced world", "Leverage", "Innovative", "Passionate", "Thought
leader", "Best practices", "Disrupt", "AI-powered" (show what it does instead of the buzzword).

### THE CLOSE & CTA
- A low-friction, genuine question, specifically targeted at operations managers/founders/decision-makers at B2B service businesses, not a generic "thoughts?" anyone could answer
- Peer-to-peer, never a command
- No external links in the body — links go in comments only

**Anatomy, named explicitly:** Hook (lines 1-2) → Context/Data (the body's factual core, delivered in short scannable lines) → Pivot/Lesson (the takeaway, what changed or what this means) → the specific CTA above. Same structure the Body/Close sections already describe, just labeled so it's checkable.

### EMOJIS
Maximum 3. Never in the first line. Only when they add clarity.

### HASHTAGS
Maximum 5, placed below the post as the last line. Every post option must ship with its
hashtag line attached, never a separate follow-up step. Choose from:
#B2BOperations #AIAutomation #BusinessAutomation #WorkflowAutomation #ScaleRise
#AIforBusiness #SalesOps #SMEgrowth #OperationsManagement

### AI IMAGE PROMPT
Every post option must ship with a ready-to-paste AI image generation prompt (for Midjourney,
DALL-E, or Canva's AI tools), not a one-line description. Write it as a full prompt someone
could paste directly into an image generator with zero editing:
- Subject and composition (what's in frame, framing/angle)
- Setting specific to the post content (an agency office, a dashboard/workflow screen, a
  founder-at-work scene, whatever the post actually calls for, never generic stock scenery)
- Mood/lighting
- Style (photorealistic, editorial photography, clean minimal illustration, whatever fits a
  professional B2B LinkedIn feed, avoid anything that reads as AI-generated slop)
- Aspect ratio note for LinkedIn (1:1 or 4:5 recommended)

Label this line "Image Prompt:" directly under the "Why:" line for that option.
